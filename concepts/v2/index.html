Questions before the first call

What does DFG do?
We help leadership teams make business decisions about technology: where to invest in AI, which vendors and partners to choose, how to launch financial products and how to answer customer security reviews.
Who do you work with?
Banks, fintechs, payment businesses and technology companies with a technology decision that affects revenue, cost or customer trust.
Do you build or implement the solution?
No. We advise on the decision and the plan. Your team or the providers you appoint build and run the solution, and we can oversee that work against the agreed plan.
How does an engagement start?
You tell us the goal and the decision in front of you. We discuss whether DFG is a fit and what information is needed, then send a written proposal.
Who works on the engagement?
The advisor who scopes the engagement stays involved until it is complete. Any specialists and their responsibilities are named in the scope.
Can we commission one service on its own?
Yes. Every service can be commissioned on its own, and an assessment never obliges you to buy further work from DFG.
Do you provide ISO certification, audits or legal opinions?
No. DFG does not provide ISO certification, independent audit opinions, legal opinions or regulated financial services.
How do you handle confidential information?
Confidentiality arrangements are agreed before sensitive documents are exchanged. Please leave confidential material out of your first enquiry.
