# DFG landing page brief (landing-page-design skill)

A concept built from the live dfg.consulting content with the landing-page-design skill. Not deployed; the live site is unchanged.

## Intake: assumptions stated

- **Primary action:** request a consultation through the enquiry form. Conversion = a submitted enquiry. There is no booking link yet.
- **Offer:** a first conversation, then a written proposal with outputs, responsibilities, fees and completion criteria before any work begins.
- **Audience:** executives and technology, product and risk leaders at banks, fintechs and technology companies facing an AI, technology, fintech launch or security decision.
- **Top objections:** (1) the advisor will push a vendor or extra work; (2) the work will be generic slides by junior staff; (3) cost and commitment are unclear.
- **Traffic:** search and referrals. Visitors know little about DFG.
- **Proof:** no client logos, testimonials, case studies or metrics have been supplied. None are invented. The proof signal uses DFG's written commitments, and the proof section shows illustrative deliverable formats, labelled as examples.
- **Risk reversal:** scope, outputs and fee agreed in writing before any work begins; every service can be commissioned on its own; free self-assessments with no email required. A free first call is not offered until the owner confirms it.
- **Voice:** professional, plain. **Direction:** minimal editorial, dark. **Mobile:** high priority.

## 1. Page outline

1. Floating navigation
2. Hero: headline, subheadline, primary CTA, proof line, district illustration
3. Problem to solution
4. Benefits (four, one per area of expertise)
5. Tagline reveal
6. How it works (three steps)
7. Proof: illustrative deliverable
8. FAQ (ten questions)
9. Risk reversal
10. Final CTA with the enquiry form
11. Footer

## 2. Hero copy

- **Headline:** Independent advisory / for AI, fintech and cybersecurity.
- **Subheadline:** Senior advisors for banks, fintechs and technology companies. We help you decide what to fund, choose the right partners and answer security reviews with evidence.
- **CTA:** Request a consultation
- **Proof line:** Senior advisor on every engagement · No referral fees · Scope and fee agreed in writing

## 3. Benefits

- **Fund the AI work that pays back.** An investment brief separates measured value from assumptions and recommends whether to pursue, test, defer or stop.
- **Choose vendors on evidence, not demonstrations.** Requirements become evaluation criteria, vendor claims are checked against references, and exit terms are compared before you sign.
- **Launch with partners that fit.** A working commercial model, a market comparison and a partner selection matrix with onboarding requirements.
- **Answer security reviews with evidence.** An evidence register and supported questionnaire responses, with the remaining gaps prioritised.

## 4. How it works

1. **Tell us the decision.** Share the objective, where things stand and any deadline.
2. **Agree the scope in writing.** A written proposal sets out outputs, responsibilities, fees and completion criteria.
3. **Receive the deliverable.** Findings and a recommendation, an implementation plan or monthly delivery reports, reviewed with your sponsor.

## 5. FAQ

1. What does DFG do?
2. Do you build or implement the solution?
3. How does an engagement start?
4. How are fees agreed?
5. Do you take referral fees from vendors or providers?
6. Who works on the engagement?
7. Can we commission one service on its own?
8. Do you provide ISO certification, audits or legal opinions?
9. How do you handle confidential information?
10. Where is DFG based?

Answers use only statements already published on dfg.consulting.

## 6. SEO and AEO

Index if adopted as the homepage. Title: "DFG | Independent advisory for AI, fintech and cybersecurity". Meta description: "Senior advisors for banks, fintechs and technology companies on AI strategy, IT strategy, fintech and payments, and cybersecurity." FAQ in plain question and answer form with FAQPage schema. The concept page itself is `noindex`.

## 7. Layout recommendation

**B, long form story.** An advisory service cannot be understood from a screenshot, and the main objections (independence, seniority, commitment) need explaining before a visitor will enquire.

## Gaps for the owner

- Client logos, a named client quote or a case study would fill the proof slots the skill expects above the fold and mid page.
- Confirm whether the first call is free; it is the strongest risk reversal available.
- The skill's visual rules replace DFG's current identity: Geist instead of Unbounded, a gradient headline and a neutral black palette. The amber accent is kept as the single accent.
