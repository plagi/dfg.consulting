# DFG content and messaging: competitor benchmark

Research date: 8 September 2026. Official company sources only. This is a selected comparison of relevant firms, not a ranking of the market or a claim that all firms compete with DFG for the same budgets. Company claims are evidence of their messaging, not independent validation of performance. Recommendations below are editorial judgments. The user has confirmed that DFG does not provide ISO certification; competitor certification and audit offerings are not recommendations to add those services to DFG.

## Core finding

The most transferable pattern is a complete argument: identify the buyer and their situation; name a decision or outcome; explain the work and resulting deliverables; show why this team can deliver; offer an appropriate next step. Professional language helps, but cannot repair missing links in that argument. DFG should make its principal-led model concrete through named accountability, bounded engagements and relevant evidence.

## What to learn from assessments

### Capco: explain the inference, not just the questions

The indexed official assessment describes 16 capability questions, scored maturity levels and mapping to 12 forms of AI value. It distinguishes an initial readiness snapshot from its fuller assessment. An observable first question uses descriptions of organisational practice at each level rather than unexplained numerical ratings. The indexed content also shows email collection before starting and a further form before full results. [Capco AI Value Readiness Assessment](https://www.capco.com/ai/value-readiness-assessment)

Observation limit: search extraction exposed assessment content, while a direct page open returned only navigation/footer content. No submission or end-to-end test was performed. The method's validity was not independently assessed.

DFG application: explain what answers can support. A short self-report can identify topics to investigate or suggest a suitable conversation. It cannot, by itself, establish implementation effort, a delivery date, commercial viability or security assurance. Results should trace recommendations to particular answers and name unresolved assumptions. Do not copy email gating just because a larger firm uses it.

### fscom: make the value exchange explicit

The homepage describes a free self-assessment taking under five minutes, then names the expected outputs: a maturity score, strengths and vulnerabilities, benchmarking and a roadmap. It also identifies the relevant financial-services sectors and displays attributed customer testimonials. The wording states what the visitor is investing and expects to receive. The underlying questionnaire and scoring were not tested. [fscom homepage](https://fscom.co/)

DFG application: choose a truthful promise for each tool. If the output is generated from a handful of answers, call it an initial self-check or indicative summary. Explain the limits and identify the paid review that would examine supporting evidence. Only promise benchmarking if there is a defensible reference population or documented framework.

## Substantive changes recommended for DFG's content logic

1. **Choose a primary commercial story.** Describe the customer, consequential decisions and DFG's role in one connected statement. AI, fintech and cybersecurity need a shared reason to appear in one practice. A possible unifier is helping leadership teams decide, prepare and oversee important technology initiatives. Confirm that this matches DFG's actual work before using it.
2. **Separate service domains from engagement stages.** Use stable pillar names across navigation, summaries, detailed sections and tools. Avoid making the customer reconcile overlapping lists of services, capabilities, solutions and packages.
3. **State a complete offer for each pillar.** Specify the trigger for hiring DFG, intended buyer, work performed, outputs, DFG/client responsibilities and next decision. For example, fintech market-entry work could produce a market comparison, partner requirements and launch dependencies; promise only outputs DFG actually delivers.
4. **Distinguish advice, coordination and delivery.** If DFG oversees external delivery teams, say who builds, who contracts with the supplier and what DFG reviews. Ambiguous verbs such as deliver, build and implement should not silently imply equivalent responsibility.
5. **Support claims with relevant evidence.** A named principal's specific role in a past engagement is more informative than unsourced breadth or superlatives. An anonymised case should still explain the organisation type, problem, DFG or principal role, work and result. Attribute prior employment separately.
6. **Make assessment conclusions proportionate to inputs.** Distinguish unknown, not applicable, absent, planned and evidenced states. A fintech company's stage or choice of operating model is context, not inherently a weakness. Security readiness depends on the reviewer's requirements and evidence quality. AI readiness depends on the proposed use case, operating context and risk.
7. **Use branching to answer different business questions.** An AI tool should help determine what to investigate or prioritise. A fintech tool should surface market, product and partnership dependencies. A security tool should organise readiness for a particular customer review. One generic score or sales conclusion should not substitute for these different decisions.
8. **Show the reasoning in results.** Use: your stated objective; what your answers indicate; what remains unknown; recommended next actions; what a consultant would need to verify. Avoid implying that all positive self-reported answers establish approval, safety or completeness.
9. **Make the next step logically follow the page.** A readiness summary should lead to a scoped discussion of its gaps; a service page should lead to a conversation about that service. Explain what the first conversation covers and what the visitor receives afterwards, using only commitments DFG can honour.

## Suggested editorial framework

Use this as a content checklist for every material section, not as identical visible headings everywhere:

> Who is this for? What situation brings them here? What decision or outcome does it support? What will DFG do? What will the customer receive? Why trust DFG with this work? What happens next?

Proposed service wording for review against actual scope:

- **AI strategy and delivery oversight:** Identify worthwhile AI applications, assess implementation requirements and oversee delivery against agreed business objectives.
- **Fintech product and market-entry advisory:** Define the proposition, assess target markets and prepare the partnership and operational requirements for launch.
- **Cybersecurity readiness and customer assurance:** Identify evidence gaps, prioritise remediation and prepare for customer security reviews.

These are proposed formulations, not verified statements of DFG capability. The commercial narrative should be confirmed before polishing every sentence or expanding the site.
