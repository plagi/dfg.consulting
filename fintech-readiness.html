Fintech readiness assessment

A short assessment of fintech readiness. The questions depend on where the product is: an idea, a prototype, a live product, or one expanding into new markets. The result is written from the answers: the open items, the questions an investor, partner or regulator is likely to ask, a suggested order of work, and whether further support would be useful.

Five or six questions that depend on where the product isAbout four minutesEvery answer explained. Nothing is stored

The full fintech assessment takes two weeks and covers six sections: the segment and the product, the model, the market and its licensing, the providers, the partners, and the plan.

What you get

The open items between the product and the business, the questions an investor, partner or regulator is likely to ask, a suggested order of work with indicative timings, and a view on whether further support would be useful.

Privacy

Nothing is stored. Answers stay in your browser until you choose to send them with a message, and then only as text you can see and edit.
