Privacy notice

Last updated 8 September 2026. Applies to dfg.consulting.

This notice says what information this website collects, why, and who else handles it. The controller is DFG Digital Frontiers FZ-LLC ("DFG", "we"). Questions go to hello@dfg.consulting.

What we collect

  • What you send through the contact form: your name, company, work email, your message, and any check result you choose to include. We use it to reply to you and to scope the work you asked about. We don't add you to a mailing list.
  • Server logs: the hosting provider records the IP address, browser type, requested page and time of each request, as every web server does. We use this only to keep the site running and secure.

What we don't collect

The site sets no cookies and runs no analytics or advertising scripts. The three checks run entirely in your browser; your answers are not sent to us unless you press the button that adds them to a message, and then only as text you can see and edit before sending.

Who else handles it

  • Cloudflare hosts the site and serves every page and form submission through its network.
  • Resend delivers form submissions to our mailbox as email.
  • Google Fonts supplies the typefaces. Your browser requests them from Google's servers, which receive your IP address as part of that request.

Each of these processes data on our behalf under its own terms. We do not sell or share your information with anyone else, and we don't use it for anything other than replying to you and keeping records of correspondence.

How long we keep it

Correspondence is kept for as long as needed to handle your enquiry and, if it leads to work, for the period our engagement records require. Server logs are retained by the hosting provider for a short rolling period.

Your rights

You can ask what we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Write to hello@dfg.consulting and we will answer within one working day. If you are in a jurisdiction that gives you further rights over your data, those rights apply and we will honour them.

Changes

If this notice changes, the date at the top changes with it. The current version is always at dfg.consulting/privacy.
