03 / The engagement

The right scope for the decision.

A focused review can stand on its own. Further work follows the decision and your needs.

Understand the options

Assessment

Review the agreed evidence, identify limitations and recommend the next decision.

Output: findings, options and recommendation.

Make the choice executable

Execution planning

Turn the chosen option into responsibilities, dependencies, budget assumptions and acceptance criteria.

Output: an agreed plan for the work.

Keep delivery accountable

Delivery oversight

Coordinate the agreed vendor reviews, report progress and escalate risks as work proceeds.

Output: reporting and decision support against the plan.

You can commission the support you need without buying every stage. Scope, access and stakeholder availability determine the schedule. Where assessment and planning are combined, the proposal defines the complete deliverable.

Working with DFG
Scope and fees
Agreed in writing before work begins, with outputs, responsibilities and completion criteria.
Senior involvement
The principal who scopes the engagement remains involved throughout. Any specialists and their responsibilities are identified in the scope.
Independence
No referral fees from providers we recommend. Existing tools and internal delivery options are considered where relevant.
Delivery
Your team and appointed providers implement the work. DFG advises and oversees the agreed scope.
Confidentiality
Confidentiality arrangements are agreed before sensitive documents are exchanged.
