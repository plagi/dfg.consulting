02

Method

Three steps, the same on every engagement, with the durations written down.

Diagnose, plan, stay. The third step is where the plan meets the vendors, the budget and the calendar. It is the one we are hired for.

Weeks 1-2DiagnoseWeeks 3-4PlanUntil it shipsStay

Diagnose

Two weeks. Interviews, systems, data, contracts. A written Diagnosis of what is actually there, what it costs and what is at risk. If the honest answer is that you don't need us, it is on page one.

Plan

Two weeks. Owners, sequence, budget bands and the vendors worth talking to. Written for the people who approve it and the people who build it, in the same document.

Stay

Until it ships. Delivery oversight, vendor by vendor, until what the plan promised is running in production and measured. Monthly, with a notice period, no lock-in. When a vendor slips, you hear it from us first.

03

Terms

How we work, in writing, before the first call.

Where
Wherever the client is. Remote by default, on site where a room is needed.
Regulation
Advisory only. We don't carry out regulated activity, hold client money or give investment advice. Where a regulated step is needed, we bring in the licensed party.
Price
Fixed scope, fixed price, in USD, agreed in writing before the start. The Diagnosis is a fixed fee; delivery oversight is a monthly retainer with a notice period. A written quote follows the scoping call within three working days.
Vendors
We select and oversee them. No resale, no implementation contracts, no referral fees from anyone we recommend. If the right answer is a tool you already own, the Diagnosis says so.
Who
The principal who scoped the work, on every engagement, start to finish. Specialists join for named tasks, a penetration test or a data-engineering sprint, and are named in the scope before you sign.
Confidentiality
A non-disclosure agreement before the first document changes hands, on your paper or ours.
Fit
If someone else is the better fit, you hear it in the first conversation, with a name. It costs nothing.
