01 / What you receive

Deliverables you can act on.

A supported decision, an implementation plan or delivered work ready for handover, depending on what you commission.

We agree the deliverables before work begins and review them with your team at handover. You can commission a focused review, implementation, or both.

AI investment and pilot reviews
An investment brief or pilot review report: value and cost assumptions, data and integration requirements, delivery options and a recommendation. Use it to fund the next step, change scope or stop, with the reasons recorded.
Fintech launch and market entry
A working commercial model and a market-entry brief: revenue, cost and unit-economic assumptions, a comparison of market and operating options, and the questions left for qualified advisers. Use it to choose a market and operating approach or defer the launch.
Partner and provider selection
A requirements-based comparison of providers, a recommended option with its trade-offs, and the questions to resolve before contracting. Use it to select a provider and plan the onboarding.
Customer security reviews
An evidence register, questionnaire responses supported by that evidence, and a prioritised gap plan for the agreed scope. Use it to respond accurately and assign follow-up work. It is not certification or a guarantee of customer approval.
When you commission implementation

Delivered work, acceptance checks and handover

The output is the agreed solution or operational change, not only a recommendation. Depending on the task, this can include configured systems, integrations, implemented workflows or security improvements, with the agreed testing, documentation and handover to the people who will operate them.

DFG performs work directly or brings in delivery partners to match the task. Before work starts, the scope identifies who delivers each part, how it will be accepted and what support is included after handover.

03 / The engagement

The right scope for the decision.

A focused review can stand on its own. Further work follows the decision and your needs.

Understand the options

Assessment

Review the agreed evidence, identify limitations and recommend the next decision.

Output: findings, options and recommendation.

Make the choice executable

Execution planning

Turn the chosen option into responsibilities, dependencies, budget assumptions and acceptance criteria.

Output: an agreed plan for the work.

Put the plan into practice

Implementation and delivery

Deliver the agreed work directly or with partners, verify it against acceptance criteria and hand it over. Where you already have a delivery team, DFG can provide oversight only.

Output: completed work, acceptance evidence and handover. For oversight-only work, progress reviews and decision support.

You can commission the support you need without buying every stage. Scope, access and stakeholder availability determine the schedule. Where assessment and planning are combined, the proposal defines the complete deliverable.

Working with DFG
Scope and fees
Agreed in writing before work begins, with outputs, responsibilities and completion criteria.
Senior involvement
The principal who scopes the engagement remains involved throughout. Any specialists and their responsibilities are identified in the scope.
Independence
No referral fees from providers we recommend. Existing tools and internal delivery options are considered where relevant. Implementation is quoted separately, and a recommendation never depends on DFG delivering it.
Delivery
DFG implements directly or with delivery partners depending on the task. The scope names the delivery responsibilities, client inputs, acceptance criteria and handover arrangements.
Confidentiality
Confidentiality arrangements are agreed before sensitive documents are exchanged.
