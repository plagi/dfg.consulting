02

Services

Three lines of work. Each starts with a Diagnosis and ends in production.

For a board with an AI mandate and no plan, a fintech choosing its market, its model and its partners, or a CTO holding a customer's due-diligence questionnaire. Every engagement starts with a two-week written Diagnosis and ends with something running in production. Fixed scope, fixed price, in writing before day one.

AI and IT consulting

The Diagnosis ranks your use-cases by value and feasibility, reviews the data and the vendors, and says which two are worth taking to production first. Then a plan with owners, sequence and budget bands, and oversight of the build until the first use-case is live and measured.

For a board with a mandate for AI and no plan, or a programme that stalled at the pilot stage. The readiness assessment on this site is the first ten minutes of that Diagnosis.

Run the readiness assessment
The Diagnosis8 sections · 20 to 40 pages
  1. 01What is there Systems, data, contracts, people. Verified, not reported.
  2. 02Who owns what Every system and dataset with a named owner, or the gap where one should be.
  3. 03The use-cases, ranked By value and by feasibility, on one page. Most lists shrink here.
  4. 04The data What it will cost to make usable, and which use-cases that removes.
  5. 05The vendors What they offer, what they charge, and what they are not telling you.
  6. 06The risks What stops pilots reaching production here, specifically.
  7. 07The plan Owners, sequence, budget bands. Written so the board can approve it.
  8. 08The recommendation Including whether you need us at all.
Always the same eight. Written for the people who approve it and the people who build it.

Fintech advisory

Product strategy, business model and unit economics, market entry and the licensing landscape, the provider ecosystem, and partnership design with banks and card networks. Bank onboarding is one step of that work, and the one most often done in the wrong order. We have sat on the bank's side of that table; we know which answer ends the conversation.

For a founder with a product and no model, a bank launching a fintech product outside the core, or a fintech choosing its sponsor bank and its providers.

The fintech Diagnosis6 sections · 20 to 40 pages
  1. 01The segment and the product With the customer evidence, not the pitch.
  2. 02The model Pricing, cost to serve, cost to acquire, payback.
  3. 03The market Licensing landscape per market, and where a licensed party is needed.
  4. 04The providers KYC, payments, core, custody: what they offer, charge, and don't say.
  5. 05The partners Banks and card networks, and the evidence each will ask for, in order.

04

Method

Three steps, with the durations written down.

Diagnose, plan, stay. The third step is where the plan meets the vendors, the budget and the calendar. It is the one we are hired for.

Weeks 1-2DiagnoseWeeks 3-4PlanUntil it shipsStay

Diagnose

Two weeks. Interviews, systems, data, contracts. A written Diagnosis of what is actually there, what it costs and what is at risk. If the honest answer is that you don't need us, it is on page one.

Plan

Two weeks. Owners, sequence, budget bands and the vendors worth talking to. Written for the people who approve it and the people who build it, in the same document.

Stay

Until it ships. Delivery oversight, vendor by vendor, until what the plan promised is running in production and measured. Monthly, with a notice period, no lock-in. When a vendor slips, you hear it from us first.

05

Terms

How we work, in writing, before the first call.

Where
Wherever the client is. Remote by default, on site where a room is needed.
Regulation
Advisory only. We don't carry out regulated activity, hold client money or give investment advice. Where a regulated step is needed, we bring in the licensed party.
Price
Fixed scope, fixed price, in USD, agreed in writing before the start. The Diagnosis is a fixed fee; delivery oversight is a monthly retainer with a notice period. A written quote follows the scoping call within three working days.
Vendors
We select and oversee them. No resale, no implementation contracts, no referral fees from anyone we recommend. If the right answer is a tool you already own, the Diagnosis says so.
Who
The principal who scoped the work, on every engagement, start to finish. Specialists join for named tasks, a penetration test or a data-engineering sprint, and are named in the scope before you sign.
Confidentiality
A non-disclosure agreement before the first document changes hands, on your paper or ours.
Fit
If someone else is the better fit, you hear it in the first conversation, with a name. It costs nothing.
