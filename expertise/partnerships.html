Services / Partner decisions

Banking partner and provider selection

Choose partners that fit your product and operating model. DFG defines requirements, compares providers and supports commercial evaluation and onboarding preparation.

Choose with the full operating relationship in view

A provider decision affects more than the initial price. Your product also depends on capabilities, reliability, integration effort, operating responsibilities and what happens if the relationship changes.

This engagement fits a team selecting a sponsor bank, payments provider, KYC service, core platform or another critical partner. It also fits a company reviewing a provider that no longer meets its requirements.

From requirements to a supported choice

  1. Define what the product needs

    Agree functional, commercial, technical and assurance requirements before comparing candidates.

  2. Evaluate the alternatives

    Compare capabilities, total cost, operational responsibilities, integration dependencies and exit arrangements. Record evidence and unresolved questions.

  3. Prepare for the relationship

    Support commercial discussions and assemble the onboarding requirements, owners and dependencies for the selected option.

What you receive

A selection matrix and recommendation

  • Requirements and evaluation criteria agreed with your team.
  • A documented comparison and the tradeoffs behind the recommendation.
  • Commercial issues and unresolved questions for negotiation and specialist review.
  • An onboarding evidence register and integration dependency plan for the agreed scope.

Clear responsibilities from the start

Bring the product requirements, markets, expected volumes, current contracts or proposals and the people who will operate the relationship. We agree the provider categories, depth of evaluation and any negotiation or onboarding support included.

DFG advises on selection and coordinates the agreed evaluation. Your organisation appoints and contracts with providers; your team and providers perform integration. Legal terms are reviewed by qualified advisers.

We receive no referral fees from providers we recommend. The engagement ends with the agreed selection and preparation deliverables, not a promise that a bank will approve an application.
