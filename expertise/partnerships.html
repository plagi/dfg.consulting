Expertise  /  04 · Fintech product, licensing and bank partnerships

Partnerships and the provider ecosystem

A fintech runs on other people's licences and infrastructure: a sponsor bank, a card network, a KYC vendor, a processor, a custodian. We design those partnerships, select the providers on terms rather than on price, and prepare the evidence each one will ask for, in the order they will ask.

Service line: Fintech product, licensing and bank partnershipsDiagnosis 2 weeksPlan 2 weeksStay until it ships

What we do

We design the partnership with the bank or the card network: what each side gives, what each side needs, and where the commercial terms usually go wrong.

We select the providers, KYC and AML, payments, core banking, custody, on a written comparison of what they offer, what they charge, and what their exit terms and assurance look like. No referral fees from any of them.

We prepare the evidence a sponsor bank's onboarding review works from, in the bank's order, and sit alongside the review. We have sat on the bank's side of that table.

Where a regulated step is needed, a licence, a legal opinion, an audit, we bring in the licensed party and manage them.

What the Diagnosis covers

  1. 01The partnership options and what each requires of you
  2. 02The provider shortlist for each function, with costs and exit terms
  3. 03The bank's evidence list against what exists today, item by item
  4. 04The integration architecture the partner's technology team will ask for
  5. 05A plan with owners and weeks for each gap

What you get

A partnership design. A provider comparison with exit terms. The bank's evidence list, prepared in its order. Oversight through selection, contracting and the review, until the partnership is signed and the integration is live.

When to call

A fintech choosing a sponsor bank or a card programme. A team replacing a KYC or payments provider. A founder facing a bank's onboarding review for the first time.
