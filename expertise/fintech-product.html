Fintech product and business model

Product strategy for fintechs: the segment, the product, the business model and the unit economics behind it, and the order in which to enter markets. Written so a founder can run it and an investor or a board can approve it.

Service line: Fintech product, licensing and bank partnershipsAssessment 2 weeksPlan 2 weeksStay until it ships

What we do

We work out which segment and which product, with the customer evidence that supports the choice.

We build the business model and the unit economics: pricing, cost to serve, cost to acquire, and the point at which the model is self-funding.

We map the market entry: the licensing landscape in each market, what needs a licensed partner, and what can launch as an unregulated product first.

We write the execution plan with owners, sequence and budget bands, and the metrics that show within a quarter whether it is working.

What the assessment covers

  1. 01The segment and the product, with the evidence for each
  2. 02Unit economics: pricing, cost to serve, cost to acquire, payback
  3. 03The licensing landscape per market, and where a licensed party is needed
  4. 04The competitors and the partners, and which are which
  5. 05A sequenced plan with owners, budget bands and the metrics to watch

What you get

A model built from measured inputs. The licensing landscape per market. A market-entry sequence. A plan with owners and metrics. Oversight through the build and the launch, monthly. Advisory only; where a regulated step is needed we bring in the licensed party.

When to call

A founder with a prototype and no pricing. A bank or corporate launching a fintech product outside the core. A board that has been shown a deck and wants the numbers behind it.
