02 / Services

Services

Each service can be commissioned on its own.

Business and commercial model

We build or review the financial model with you: revenue, cost and unit-economic assumptions, and the sensitivities that would change the decision.

You receive
  • A working commercial model with its assumptions documented.
  • The sensitivities that matter most and how to test them.

Market entry assessment

We compare target markets on customer demand, operating requirements and partner availability, and identify the questions that need qualified legal or regulatory advice.

You receive
  • A comparison of market and operating options.
  • A market-entry brief with validation gaps and partner dependencies.
  • The questions to take to qualified advisers.

Banking and provider partner selection

We define what the product needs from a banking, payment or service partner, build the shortlist and compare the options on capability, cost, operating responsibilities and exit.

You receive
  • A selection matrix and a supported recommendation.
  • Onboarding requirements and integration dependencies.

Launch planning

We connect the customer proposition to the operating model, providers and dependencies the launch requires.

You receive
  • A launch plan with owners, dependencies and decision points.

04 / Scope

Scope and limits

What to bring
The proposition, the target customers, any existing model or plan and the partner conversations already under way.
What we do not do
We are not a law firm and do not give legal or regulatory opinions. We prepare the questions and work alongside your qualified advisers. Partners make their own approval decisions.
When the work is complete
When the agreed deliverables have been reviewed with your sponsor. Completion never depends on you buying further work from DFG.
