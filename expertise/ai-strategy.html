AI strategy and delivery

An organisation has decided to use AI and has not decided how. The work is to rank the candidate use-cases, fix only what the first two depend on, choose and contract the vendors, and oversee the build until the first use-case runs in production with a measured result.

Service line: AI strategy and deliveryDiagnosis 2 weeksPlan 2 weeksStay until it ships

What we do

We rank the candidate use-cases by value and by feasibility, on one page, with a business owner named for each. Most lists of thirty become a list of two.

We review the data those two depend on and the vendors who would supply the models: what they offer, what they charge, and what the contract does not say.

We write the plan with owners, sequence and budget bands, so the board can approve it and the people who build it can follow it.

We oversee the build, vendor by vendor, until the first use-case runs in production and its result is reported to the sponsor. Monthly, with a notice period.

What the Diagnosis covers

  1. 01Executive ownership: who is accountable, and whether there is a budget line
  2. 02The two or three datasets the first use-cases need, and what it costs to make them usable
  3. 03A ranked shortlist with a named business owner for each candidate
  4. 04What is built in-house and what is bought, and the vendor terms that let your team operate it
  5. 05A review path that approves a use-case in weeks, with a record an auditor can read

What you get

A ranked shortlist. A plan with owners, sequence and budget bands. Vendor terms your team can operate. A monthly written status until the first use-case is live and measured.

When to call

A board with a mandate for AI and no plan. A CTO whose pilots stop before production. A sponsor who needs a measured result before the next budget round.
