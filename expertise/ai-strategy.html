AI strategy and delivery

A board has a mandate for AI and no plan, or a programme that stalled at the pilot stage. The work is to choose the two use-cases worth taking to production, fix only what they depend on, and stay until the first one is live and measured.

Service line: AI and IT consultingDiagnosis 2 weeksPlan 2 weeksStay until it ships

Thirty candidates become two

We rank the candidate use-cases by value and by feasibility, on one page. Most lists of thirty become a list of two.

We review the data those two depend on and the vendors who would supply the models, and say what each is not telling you.

We write a plan with owners, sequence and budget bands, so the board can approve it and the people who build it can follow it.

Then we oversee the build, vendor by vendor, until the first use-case is running in production with a result the sponsor can report.

The Diagnosis, for AI

  1. 01Executive ownership: who is accountable, and whether there is a budget line
  2. 02The two or three datasets the first use-cases need, and what it costs to make them usable
  3. 03A ranked shortlist with a named business owner for each candidate
  4. 04What is built in-house and what is bought, and the vendor terms that let your team operate it
  5. 05A review path that approves a use-case in weeks, with a record an auditor can read

After the board says yes

Delivery oversight, monthly with a notice period, until the first use-case is in production and measured. When a vendor slips, you hear it from us first.

Who calls us

A board with a mandate for AI and no plan. A CTO whose pilots keep stopping. A sponsor who needs evidence before the next budget round.
