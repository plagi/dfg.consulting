02 / Services

Services

Each service can be commissioned on its own.

AI investment assessment

We review the process you want to improve, compare candidate use cases and examine data, integration and risk requirements. Existing tools and non-AI alternatives are considered alongside build and buy options.

You receive
  • A value case that separates measured inputs from assumptions.
  • Feasibility and delivery options, with dependencies and cost assumptions.
  • A recommendation to pursue, test, defer or stop.

AI pilot review

We start from the pilot’s actual results and the reasons deployment stopped. The review covers success criteria, data and integration constraints, production costs and operating responsibilities.

You receive
  • A findings register linked to the pilot evidence.
  • Options to continue, change scope or stop.
  • Where continuation is supported, the dependencies and acceptance criteria for production planning.

Data readiness review

Scoped to one initiative. We review whether the required datasets are accessible and suitable, how the systems will connect and who owns each dependency.

You receive
  • A dependency map for the use case.
  • The changes needed before a delivery commitment can be made.

04 / Scope

Scope and limits

What to bring
The business objective, any pilot results or supplier proposals, current cost assumptions and access to the people responsible for the process, data and technology.
What we do not do
We advise on the decision and the plan. Your team or the providers you appoint build and run the solution, and we can oversee that work against the agreed plan.
When the work is complete
When the agreed deliverables have been reviewed with your sponsor. Completion never depends on you buying further work from DFG.
