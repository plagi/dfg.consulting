Data and IT foundations

Most AI plans fail on the ground beneath them: nobody owns the dataset, the architecture is in three people's heads, and the contract has no exit. We write down what is actually there, verified rather than reported, and what to fix before anything is built on it.

Service line: AI and IT consultingDiagnosis 2 weeksPlan 2 weeksStay until it ships

Verified, not reported

We inventory systems, data, contracts and people, and check each claim against the thing itself.

We name an owner for every system and dataset that matters, or record the gap where one should be.

We measure the quality of the data a plan depends on, not the whole estate, and price what it takes to make it usable.

We write the integration architecture down: APIs, environments, responsibilities, and the change process.

What the Diagnosis records

  1. 01Every system and dataset with a named owner, or the gap
  2. 02The cost to make the needed data usable, and which use-cases that removes
  3. 03Contracts, their terms and their exits
  4. 04Tools you already own that do the job, so you do not buy them twice
  5. 05The architecture, written so a new engineer can read it

The sprints that follow

Data-engineering sprints and architecture changes are named in the scope before you sign, with specialists named for each. We oversee them until the measured quality is reached.

Who calls us

A CTO who has inherited an estate. A board about to approve an AI budget. A fintech whose enterprise customer wants the architecture in writing.
