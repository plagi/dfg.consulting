Security posture and customer due diligence

A customer, a partner or the board asks how secure the company is and wants an answer with evidence behind it. We assess the posture against the frameworks they ask about, ISO 27001, SOC 2 and PCI DSS among them, review the architecture and the vendors, and stay for the remediation until the evidence exists. We prepare you for reviews and audits; we do not perform audits.

Service line: Security posture and customer due diligenceDiagnosis 2 weeksPlan 2 weeksStay until it ships

What we do

We assess the controls in the order a reviewer works through them: governance, access, data, suppliers and cloud, operations, resilience. In place means written, operating, and evidenced.

We review the architecture and the vendors that hold your data, and what their assurance actually covers.

We write the gap list in the reviewer's order, with an owner and a number of weeks for each item, and what to accept rather than fix.

We answer the due-diligence questionnaire with you, with evidence or an honest date behind each answer, and oversee the remediation vendor by vendor until the evidence exists.

What the Diagnosis covers

  1. 01Scope and assets: what holds customer data, and who owns it
  2. 02The risk assessment, with what is accepted written down
  3. 03Controls set against ISO 27001, SOC 2 and PCI DSS, as your customers ask
  4. 04Architecture: identity, network, data, keys
  5. 05Suppliers and cloud: who holds your data, and their assurance
  6. 06Incident response: the plan, the rehearsal, the notifications

What you get

A gap list with owners and weeks. Architecture and vendor review with the risks named. Questionnaire answers with evidence or dates. Remediation oversight, monthly, until the customer signs, and until the audit passes if one follows.

When to call

A CTO whose largest customer has sent a security questionnaire. A COO facing a partner's third-party review. A board that wants a straight answer on where the estate stands.
