02 / Services

Services

Each service can be commissioned on its own.

Security posture review

We review policies, controls, supplier responsibilities and technical documentation for the agreed scope, and separate evidence that exists from controls that need work.

You receive
  • A findings register with the evidence behind each finding.
  • A prioritised remediation plan with owners.

Customer security review support

We work from the actual questionnaire or review request, establish the scope and organise the evidence behind each answer.

You receive
  • A scope statement and an evidence register linked to the request.
  • Questionnaire responses that separate evidence, limitations and open actions.
  • A prioritised gap plan.

Incident preparedness

We review response roles, escalation and critical-service dependencies, and can run a facilitated exercise that tests how people make decisions under pressure.

You receive
  • Agreed roles and escalation paths.
  • Exercise findings and the actions that improve the plan.

04 / Scope

Scope and limits

What to bring
The review request, the service boundary, available records and the people responsible for security, technology and suppliers.
What we do not do
We do not provide ISO certification, independent audit opinions or emergency incident response. Where technical testing such as a penetration test is needed, we define the scope and help you appoint a specialist.
When the work is complete
When the agreed deliverables have been reviewed with your sponsor. Completion never depends on you buying further work from DFG.
