Security posture and audit readiness

Posture assessment against ISO 27001 and the frameworks your customers ask about, an architecture and vendor review, and a gap list in the order an auditor works through it. Then we stay for the remediation, vendor by vendor.

Service line: Cybersecurity consultingDiagnosis 2 weeksPlan 2 weeksStay until it ships

In the order an auditor works

We assess the controls an auditor samples first: policy, inventory, access, suppliers, incidents, then the technical controls.

We review the architecture and the vendors that hold your data, and what their assurance actually covers.

We write the gap list in audit order, so the first item is the one to close first.

We oversee remediation with each vendor and each internal owner until the evidence exists, not only the document.
