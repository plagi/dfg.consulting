Services / Security decisions

Customer security review support

Prepare clear, supportable answers to customer security reviews. DFG helps you understand the request, organise evidence and address the gaps that matter to the agreed scope.

Turn the request into a manageable scope

A customer or partner has asked how you protect the service they will use. The first task is to establish what they are reviewing, what evidence you hold and what remains unresolved.

We work from the actual questionnaire or review request where available. We distinguish evidence that exists, controls that need work and questions that require further investigation.

Make each answer supportable

  1. Establish the scope

    Identify the service, systems, information and supplier responsibilities relevant to the request.

  2. Review the evidence

    Organise policies, records and technical documentation against the agreed questions. Record what the evidence supports and its limitations.

  3. Resolve what matters

    Support accurate responses and prioritise substantiated gaps by their impact and the reviewer’s requirements. Agree owners for further work.

What you can take into the review

You receive

An evidence register and supported responses

  • A scope statement and evidence register linked to the request.
  • Questionnaire responses that distinguish evidence, limitations and open actions.
  • A prioritised gap plan with responsibilities and dependencies.

Where needed, remediation oversight is separately scoped. Your team or appointed specialists implement the changes, while DFG coordinates the agreed reporting and review.

Illustrative work sample

An evidence register that distinguishes facts from gaps

Review question
How is access removed when someone leaves?
Evidence available
An access-removal procedure.

Next action

When the review raises incident preparedness

We can include response roles, escalation, critical-service dependencies and a facilitated exercise in the scope. The exercise tests how people would make decisions and identifies actions to improve the plan.

A discussion exercise does not establish that a technical recovery works. Restore testing and other technical work remain with the responsible team or an appointed specialist. This is preparedness advice, not an emergency response service.

What this engagement establishes

Bring the review request, the service boundary, available records and the people responsible for security, technology and suppliers. The scope states which evidence is reviewed and what further specialist work may be needed.

DFG provides advisory and evidence-preparation support. We do not provide ISO certification or independent audit opinions. Any framework references apply only to the agreed review; the engagement does not certify compliance or guarantee a customer’s decision.

Completion means the agreed evidence review, responses and action plan have been delivered and discussed.
